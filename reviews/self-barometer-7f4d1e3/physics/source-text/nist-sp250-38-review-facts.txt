The gas state uses PV=NRT. Molecular-flow conductance relates molar flow to pressure difference divided by RT. Viscous-flow conductance depends on pressure and geometry. The two-volume relaxation has tau=V1*V2/[C*(V1+V2)]. A large receiving volume gives tau approximately V1/C. This is vacuum-leak metrology, not measurements of ceramic walls or tarred joints at atmospheric pressure.
